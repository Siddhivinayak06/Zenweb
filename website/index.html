About ZenWeb

What it is: ZenWeb is a Chrome extension that simplifies web pages—removing clutter, enabling focus modes, and offering AI summaries and assistive tools.

Who it's for: People with ADHD, dyslexia, autism, chronic fatigue, students, and professionals who need a calmer web experience.

Why cognitive-first accessibility matters: The web should adapt to people. ZenWeb puts neurodivergent and cognitively disabled users at the core, following WCAG guidelines and respecting reduced-motion preferences.
