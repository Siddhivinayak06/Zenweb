Why ZenWeb?

The Problem

Modern websites bombard users with pop-ups, autoplay videos, flashing ads, and dense layouts. For people with cognitive differences, this creates anxiety, distraction, and mental overload—making simple browsing tasks exhausting.

Our Solution

ZenWeb strips away the noise. Simplify mode declutters pages. Focus mode highlights what matters. AI summaries, dyslexia-friendly fonts, and keyboard shortcuts put you in control. Browse the web on your terms.

3

Browse with Reduced Cognitive Load

Built for Everyone

ZenWeb is designed with neurodivergent and cognitively disabled users at the core. We believe the web should adapt to people—not the other way around. Our features follow WCAG guidelines, support screen readers, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
